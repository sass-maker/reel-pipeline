# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the Fleet operator producing short product and campaign
videos from approved source material. They need a fast path from evidence and
assets to an editor-ready review cut without operating a general-purpose video
editor or managing several incompatible generation tools by hand. They may
create the task directly or ask an AI agent to create it, but both paths use
the same shared queue, film-skill registry, and approval history.

## Product Purpose

Reel Pipeline turns approved briefs, product evidence, generated shots, voice,
and deterministic graphics into reproducible short-form media. Success means
publishing consistently while preserving every source, prompt, model revision,
timing decision, license, and output hash.

## Positioning

One story-first production pipeline that can use many visual tools without
turning the result into a tool demonstration.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary action: render and review an approved short.
- Control surface: prompt a film, choose a versioned Film style, inspect
  source rights, monitor either machine, and decide between variants.
- Secondary action: export the editor-ready package for external finishing.
- The line to remember: `Use every weapon. Show one idea.`
- Belief ladder: the source is approved; the story is coherent; the chosen
  media is reproducible; the output is safe to publish.
- Proof on hand: local LTX generation receipts, render manifests, review
  galleries, and the CodeVetter evidence-beam reference film.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, precise, and composed. The pipeline should feel like a small
production studio with excellent editorial judgment, not an AI-effects demo.

## Anti-references

- Capability montages that expose every available tool.
- Tiny terminal panels, dashboard collages, or technology-themed decoration.
- Generic talking-head templates and unrelated mouth motion.
- Cyberpunk neon, indiscriminate acid green, glassmorphism, and floating-screen
  hologram clichés.

## Design Principles

- Story chooses the tools.
- Prompts provide intent; versioned Film styles preserve production judgment.
- Voice and typed intake compile to the same visible eight-stage workflow;
  operators can run or retry one stage, pause quick progression, and inspect
  retained checkpoints without bypassing readiness checks.
- Reusable characters, workflow-specific cast continuity, and soundtrack
  transforms stay editable before generation and remain part of the evidence.
- App-demo capture is a guided workflow; its selected Film style preserves the
  composition, synchronization, provenance, and delivery rules.
- One dominant visual idea per moment.
- Real product evidence outranks generated approximation.
- Generated motion creates atmosphere and transition, never product claims.
- Every publishable asset carries provenance and license posture.
- Generated music remains unavailable until its pinned local canary has passed
  a human listening review; the product must show that blocker explicitly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Every film includes readable captions, safe title margins, strong contrast, and
a reduced-motion review fallback. Meaning cannot depend on color alone.
